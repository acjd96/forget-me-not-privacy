Forget Me Not: Support

Forget Me Not helps you stay close to the people who matter. Choose how often you'd like to be in touch with each person, and the app shows everyone on an orbit with a ring that empties as time passes.

Requirements

An iPhone on iOS 27 or later. Conversation starters, goal tips and reading messy calendar titles use Apple Intelligence, where your iPhone has it; everything else works on any supported iPhone.

Common questions

How do I add people?

The first time you open the app, choose “Choose contacts” and sort each person into a corner: weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly. Later, use “Sort new people” from the ⋯ menu in the list at the bottom of the Orbit, or the + button to add someone by hand. You can fine-tune anyone's cadence on their page.

How do I log a catch-up?

  • Each evening, answer “Who did you see today?”. Long-press the notification to tap faces without opening the app.
  • Use “Catch up on your week” from the ⋯ menu, or the Sunday notification.
  • Tap a person, then Log contact. Or swipe right on them in the list.
  • Add the “Who did you see?” control to Control Center, the Lock Screen or the Action button.
  • Ask Siri: “Log a catch-up in Forget Me Not”, or “Log a call with Sam in Forget Me Not”.

Why didn't I get a reminder?

  • Check notifications are allowed: iOS Settings → Notifications → Forget Me Not.
  • A reminder comes once, on the day someone becomes overdue, at the time set in the app's Settings. At most three a day; if more people are due, you get one summary.
  • If nothing has been logged for a week, reminders pause and the app offers to help you catch up instead. Logging anything brings them back.
  • A Focus mode may be holding notifications back.

How do I change or turn off the evening check-in or weekly review?

In the app's Settings, under Check-in and Weekly review. You can change the time (and the day, for the weekly review) or switch either off.

How does iCloud sync work?

It's optional and off unless you choose it. When it's on, your people and history are kept in your own private iCloud, so they come back if you reinstall or get a new iPhone. Turn it on or off in the app's Settings → iCloud. Turning it off deletes the copy in iCloud; everything stays on your iPhone.

How do I remove someone?

Archive them from their page (⋯ → Archive) or by swiping left in the list. Archived people can then be deleted permanently, with their history.

Can you see my data?

No. Your people, notes and history stay on your iPhone (and in your own private iCloud, if you turn sync on). There are no accounts, servers or analytics. See the privacy policy.

Can I see the introduction again?

Yes: the app's Settings → Show the introduction.
